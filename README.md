# Open Graph Console

An independent product concept built for the AI Product Manager role at Open (Open Agentic), the team building the Global Product Graph for insurance.

Every Open Agentic product stands on the graph: structured, current, explainable product data that AI agents can reason over safely. Open Graph Console is the workbench I would run it from: coverage as a product surface, the accuracy operation, scoring as code, distribution channels, and the safety gauntlet.

Live demo: https://open-graph-console.ayomideahmedcp.workers.dev

All names, numbers and records in the demo are illustrative sample data. This is an unofficial concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by Open.
